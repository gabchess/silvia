# Silvia — Ela conversa. Maria decide. — Brief

## Audience and desired response

Hackathon judges must feel Maria regain independence, understand that Silvia
waits for explicit consent, see a real working proof, and remember: “Ela
conversa. Maria decide.”

## Product truth and proof

Silvia converts Maria’s Portuguese voice request into a priced demo order,
reads it back, and waits for confirmation. The five-second proof uses the real
Base44 app with a fictional restaurant and cash on delivery. The courier beat
is a future product vision and carries a persistent bilingual label. The film
does not claim a live iFood purchase, partnership, or deployed commerce
connector.

## Format and distribution

The primary master is a 40–45 second UGC ad for YouTube, X, and the Base44
hackathon submission. It is 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps, with Portuguese
performance and manually timed burned English captions.

## Sources, rights, budget, and deadline

Maria, the apartment, courier, and delivery are synthetic Higgsfield sources.
The Base44 proof is first-party project footage. Voice uses a licensed
Higgsfield preset. Music and SFX require a complete rights ledger. Higgsfield
spend is capped at 180 credits; HeyGen and ChatCut spend is zero. The production
deadline is 2026-07-28.
