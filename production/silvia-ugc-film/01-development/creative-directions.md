# Creative Directions

## A — Dinner without dependence

Maria arrives exhausted, cannot rely on her daughter to operate a delivery
app, discovers Silvia through WhatsApp, confirms a real demo order, and enjoys
the imagined delivery. This is the approved direction because it turns one
specific pain into visible relief.

## B — The daughter’s side

A daughter worries that her mother cannot order safely and watches a consent
notification. Rejected because it makes the caregiver, not Maria, the hero.

## C — Interface gauntlet

Maria fights a wall of app screens before one voice note clears the noise.
Rejected because generated UI would weaken truth and make the film feel like a
motion-graphics deck.
