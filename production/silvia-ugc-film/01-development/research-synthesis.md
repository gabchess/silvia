# Research Synthesis

locations, one action per generated shot, one-variable retries, and keeper
editing. The film does not copy the reference creator’s cast, plot, dialogue,
product, choreography, signature shots, or visual identity.

promise is autonomy with explicit consent. The physical and emotional change
matters more than a feature list, while the real Base44 proof carries the
