# Silvia master v2: locked English voice-over

## One idea

Maria can order dinner through a familiar conversation without giving up control.

## Voice-over

I'm Maria. I'm 72, and I live in Brazil.

I just got home from work, and I'm exhausted.

My daughter knows how to order food on those apps, but she's not home.

I just want to order dinner without having to ask her for help.

That's when I found Silvia.

So I send her a voice note: "Silvia, I'd like grilled salmon with vegetables and an orange juice."

She reads my order back, shows me the total, and waits for me to say yes.

Yes.

She talks. I decide.
